## 1. Why this policy exists

AI can save bankers hours each day. It can also leak a client's deal, invent a number in a pitchbook or breach market abuse rules. This policy tells you what you can and cannot do.

## 2. The five rules

1. **Use only approved AI tools.** Kestrel Assist, Microsoft 365 Copilot and the specialist tools on the Approved AI Tools list. Public chatbots are blocked, and using them through personal devices for work is a disciplinary matter.
2. **Never put MNPI or client data into anything other than an approved tool.** Not even "anonymised". Codenames and partial details can still identify a deal.
3. **You own the output.** Check every fact, figure and source before you use it. "The AI said so" is not a defence.
4. **Do not let AI cross the wall.** Do not ask AI tools about deals you are not on, or about names on the restricted or watch list.
5. **Report problems straight away.** If you think data went to the wrong place or a wrong AI output went to a client, tell AI Risk within one hour at `ai-incident@kestrelpoint.example`.

## 3. What you can do

| Activity | Allowed? | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Summarise public news, research and filings | Yes | Any approved tool |
| Draft pitchbook sections, teasers, CIMs | Yes | Kestrel Assist only. Human review and Compliance pre-use review before sending |
| Review VDR documents | Yes | VaultLens through the gateway. Deal team members only |
| Build or check model formulas and VBA/Python | Yes | Kestrel Assist. Model goes on the EUC register if used for a client deliverable |
| Record and transcribe meetings | Yes | NoteStream or Teams. All participants told at the start; external participants can opt out |
| Draft outreach emails to executives | Yes | Public information only about the person. Supervisory approval for templates |
| Use AI outputs to make trading decisions | Only UC-11 users | Through SignalCurrent, logged for surveillance |
| Use AI risk or credit model outputs | Only Risk | Only validated models on the model inventory |

## 4. What you cannot do

- Paste MNPI, client data or personal data into any unapproved AI tool.
- Use AI to create content that claims to be a person, or to impersonate a client or colleague.
- Send AI-generated content to clients without the review set out in the [Output Review Standard](03-output-review-and-human-oversight-standard.md).
- Use AI to profile individuals beyond their public professional information.
- Change an approved prompt in the prompt library without following the [Prompt Library Governance Standard](04-prompt-library-governance-standard.md).
- Connect AI tools to new data sources, plug-ins or agents without approval.
- Use AI to scrape websites in breach of their terms of use.

## 6. Training

Everyone completes the **AI Essentials** e-learning (45 minutes) before getting access to Kestrel Assist, and again every year. Front-office staff also complete **AI in Deals** (30 minutes). This meets the EU AI Act Article 4 AI literacy duty for the Frankfurt entity and is applied group-wide.

## 7. Monitoring

AI use on firm systems is logged and monitored by AI Risk, Compliance Surveillance and Information Security, as explained in the employee privacy notice.

## 8. Breaches

Breaches are handled under the Disciplinary Policy. Breaches involving MNPI are also reviewed by Compliance under the Market Abuse Policy and may be reported to regulators.

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions need a written request to AI Governance, a risk assessment and compensating controls. The AI Governance Lead can approve exceptions for up to 90 days. Longer exceptions go to the AIGC.
