| Field | Value |

## 1. Headline

AI is now used safely across the deal lifecycle at Kestrel. **1,080 staff** use Kestrel Assist each month, and the programme has delivered the five things the Board asked for in March. **One risk remains outside appetite:** staff can still reach public AI tools through unmanaged browser profiles and personal devices (R-01). A fix is due by 15 December 2026.

## 2. Decisions requested

1. **Note** the residual risk profile and the four control findings.
2. **Approve** the remediation plan for R-01, including mandatory device policy changes for all front-office laptops.
3. **Note** that the SignalCurrent vendor will be replaced if model documentation is not received by 31 January 2027.

## 6. Incidents

One Sev-2 incident (AI-INC-2026-004): Copilot showed a draft board paper for Project HERON to a pilot user not on the deal, because the deal site was shared too widely. It was contained within 2 hours. Compliance confirmed no trading and updated the insider list. No regulatory notification was required. Two Sev-3 incidents were caught by existing controls.
